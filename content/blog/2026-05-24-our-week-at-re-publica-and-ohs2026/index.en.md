Mai was a big months for Open.Make as we co-organised the OHS26 (open hardware summit) meeting,
providing the room at the TU Berlin, and participated to re:publica 2026.
In both events, we came as both OpenMake and 
[LAUDS Factories](https://lauds.eu) team members, 
discussing the importance of open source hardware both for academia 
and for new economic system.

## Presentation: LAUDS Factories x Open.Make

OHS 2026 started with our presentation where we joined with the SUPSI team to present the 
importance of including manufacturing concerns in the design of open source (research) hardware.
With the concept of beautiful factories, we presented evidence of the possiblitly 
to include the creation of a business without loosing one's value of openness,
inclusivity, diversity or solidarity.


## Discussion on documentation practices


### For whom and for what ?

During the unconference part of OSHWA on Sunday, 
we had a short (30 minutes) discussion on 
open hardware documentation practices.
We started by enumerating the goal of a documentation and ended up with the following list:

- For the author:
    - Continuous development (document for future self)
    - Handle versioning: knowing what is the (latest) stable version
    - Certification help: documenting quality control aspects is specialy important.
    - build and organise collaborations
    - keep track of design intend (why the design is how it is).
- For the reader: 
    - Replication of hardware: figure out very quickly if I can replicate, and replicate
    - understand things to design something different

There are different aspects to the documentation.
It is use as a communication tool. When user manuals are part of the documentation,
it is also a link to the final users.

### Where, When ?

In short: as soon as possible, on a Git forge.

Git forges seem to be the go-to place.
CAD version control systems are all proprietary, and do not bridge with Git 
(but maybe for unshape: https://www.onshape.com/en/)

Different type of documentation are needed at different times.


There may be issues with patenting.
(When fighting against the university wanting to patent things,
writing academic journal papers is way to work).

### Other

We need to expose the advantage of good documentation.
Documentation is something useful !

### Final words

This discussion confirmed our approach with the hardware documentation guide and templates,
especially the idea to make it incremental and linked to Git forges workflows.
It might be interesting to categorise the elements of the guide in terms of goals 
and motivation of the authors.

During one of the final panel session, Enrico Bassi represented the LAUDS Factories project
in the discussion of business creation and manufacturing.
The crowd was surprisingly very open to business creation, and to the idea of scaling up
production beyond single, artistic units.
